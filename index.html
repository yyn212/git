The AI Humanitarian Resource Allocation System is designed to support effective and timely distribution of humanitarian resources during flood disasters in Myanmar. The system uses artificial intelligence to analyze population, disaster impact, resource demand, emergency reports, vulnerability, accessibility, and available inventory. Based on these factors, it predicts the required resources, determines priority levels, and recommends an optimized allocation plan for affected areas. During flood disasters, humanitarian organizations often face difficulties in determining where resources are needed most, what types of resources are required, and how available resources should be distributed. Limited information, changing conditions, transportation difficulties, and unequal resource availability can lead to delays or inefficient distribution. Therefore, an intelligent system is needed to analyze multiple data sources and provide data-driven recommendations for humanitarian resource allocation. The final output of the system will be an interactive humanitarian response dashboard. The dashboard will allow users to view affected areas, predicted resource demand, priority levels, available inventory, recommended allocations, transportation routes, and volunteer recommendations. Emergency reports and weather information can also be incorporated as additional inputs. This dashboard is intended to provide humanitarian organizations with a clear overview of the situation and support faster, more informed decision-making during flood response operations.
